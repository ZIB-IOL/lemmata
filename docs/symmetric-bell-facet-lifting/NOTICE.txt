Facet lifting and frequency certificates for symmetric Bell polytopes
Lemmata entry symmetric-bell-facet-lifting

Copyright 2026 Sebastian Pokutta

This entry builds on the Lemmata entry "A three-party Bell polytope that is not
a cross-polytope" (symmetric-bell-cross-polytope, lemmata:2611.00002) by Haobo Ma
and Wenlin Zhang, which adapts the Trureturing formalization of The Omega
Institute. It imports, and does not copy, that entry's signed action, local
polytope and projection definitions and its nineteen-extreme-point theorem.
The fixed-space, face and certificate arguments are new in this entry.
