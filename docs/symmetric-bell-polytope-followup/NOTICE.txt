Facet lifting and frequency certificates for symmetric Bell polytopes
Contributor: Sebastian Pokutta

This entry imports the SymmetricBellCrossPolytope formalization by Haobo Ma
and Wenlin Zhang. The proof sumFour in Chart.lean specializes their
finite-product reindexing to four settings. The signed action, local polytope
and projection definitions and the nineteen-extreme-point theorem are
imported from their entry. The new fixed-space, face and certificate
arguments are developed here.
